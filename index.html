About

I turn raw data into decisions businesses can act on. I'm a Business Data Analytics undergraduate at COMSATS University Islamabad and a Google Business Intelligence Professional Certificate holder, building a career at the intersection of data, strategy, and storytelling.

My training covers the complete BI workflow — from gathering stakeholder requirements and structuring data with SQL, to building ETL pipelines, to designing Tableau and Power BI dashboards that answer real business questions.

What sets my approach apart is the last mile: translating metrics into clear narratives that non-technical decision-makers can actually use. A dashboard no one understands is just decoration; I build ones that drive action.

I am building my career through analytics coursework, industry certifications, internships, volunteering, and hands-on projects that solve practical problems.
